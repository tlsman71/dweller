# Field testing Dweller

Dweller is not ready for testers yet. We are in Phase 1 (checking data sources). This page explains how testing will work so partners and future testers know what to expect.

## Who we're looking for
Adults living in Mecklenburg County, NC, in Charlotte, Cornelius, Davidson, Huntersville, Matthews, Mint Hill, Pineville or unincorporated areas. A mix of ages, neighborhoods, incomes and comfort with technology.

## What testers will do
1. Try a short task in an early version (for example: onboarding, or reading one "how this affects me" story).
2. Tell us what was confusing, wrong, missing or felt slanted.
3. Each round takes 15–30 minutes.

## How to send feedback
- **On GitHub:** [open a feedback form](https://github.com/tlsman71/dweller/issues/new/choose) (needs a free GitHub account). Feedback posted here is **public**, so don't include your name, address, phone number or anything personal.
- **By email:** DwellerApp@proton.me if you'd rather not post publicly.

## What we will never ask testers for
Your party, how you vote, your religion, medical details, or your street address. Every question is optional.
