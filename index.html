Dweller

How public decisions shape your life: past, present and the next two years.

A nonpartisan civic app for North Carolina. Every claim is tied to a dated, primary source.

Early development · Pilot area: Mecklenburg County, NC

Your world

Dweller frames everything around what matters to you, today and tomorrow. You choose what to share, and every question can be skipped.

Needs

The basics decisions touch first.

  • Housing and income
  • Food, transportation
  • Health coverage, child care

Wants

Where you're trying to go.

  • Career growth, training
  • Home improvements
  • Community and belonging

Desires

Open-ended, and defined by you: the one thing you'd change in the next two years.

Logic, credibility and care meet in your digital self

Facts sit at the center. Credibility and personal meaning orbit around them.

Logos: evidence

Bills, rulings, ordinances and data, each with its primary source and date. Fact, estimate and forecast are labeled separately.

Ethos: credibility

Who decided, what they did and where it's recorded. Neutral facts about officials, never grades.

Pathos: meaning

What it means for your needs, wants and desires, in your own words.

Our commitments

These are built in from the first line of code.

NonpartisanNo endorsements or grades. Dweller never asks your party or how you vote.
SourcedEvery impact claim cites a primary source and date, with ranges instead of false precision.
PrivateEvery field is optional. Export or delete everything. Data is never sold. Read more.
