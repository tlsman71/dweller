Dweller · North Carolina

See how decisions shape your life.

Dweller is a nonpartisan civic app. It shows each resident how past and pending local, state and federal decisions affect their own needs, wants and goals, with every claim tied to a dated, primary source.

Early development · Not yet available to the public · Pilot area: Mecklenburg County, NC

What it does

Your status, explained

How laws, rulings and ordinances already on the books shape where you are today.

The next 24 months

Pending bills, rules, court cases and elections that could affect you, shown with ranges, not guesses.

Reach the right office

Dweller drafts a report and finds the right agency. You review it and send it yourself.

Help nearby

Local nonprofits, such as food pantries, can reach residents by area and topic. They never see who you are.

Our commitments

  • Nonpartisan. Neutral facts about officials and decisions. No grades, no endorsements, and Dweller never asks your party or how you vote.
  • Sourced. Every impact claim cites its primary source and date, and labels fact, estimate and forecast separately.
  • Private. Every field is optional. You can export or delete everything. Your data is never sold. See PRIVACY.md.

Where the project is

  1. Verify data sources (Mecklenburg County and its six towns)
  2. Design the profile and impact model
  3. Hand-test impact stories on 10 real NC decisions and 3 sample residents
  4. Clickable prototype
  5. Pilot in one city with one partner

Each phase must pass its test before the next one starts.

Field testers in North Carolina

We'll be looking for residents of Charlotte, Cornelius, Davidson, Huntersville, Matthews, Mint Hill, Pineville and unincorporated Mecklenburg to try early versions and tell us what's confusing, wrong or missing.
