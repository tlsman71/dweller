Our commitments

Nonpartisan

Neutral facts about officials: votes, bills, status. No grades or endorsements. We never ask your party or vote.

Sourced

Every impact claim cites a primary source and date, with ranges instead of false precision.

Private

Every field optional. Export or delete everything. Never sold. No medical readings. Details
