The Street

Two ways to get Medicare sit on either side of one street. Each scroll step is one part of the 2024 presentation. The figures stand for average people, never real ones.

On the left side of the street: Original Medicare, run by CMS, with an hourglass for the Part A trust fund. On the right side: buildings for the Medicare Advantage insurers UnitedHealthcare, Humana, Aetna (CVS Health), Elevance, Kaiser Permanente, Centene (WellCare), and all other plans.

Company names are text labels, not logos. Building sizes are a drawing for now; they will follow each company’s Medicare Advantage enrollment once those figures are verified.

Tap or click any circle to see its record: every connection, with the document it comes from.

How to read this

  1. Click a circle to see its record, in date order, each with its source.
  2. Solid green lines are documented money or facts.
  3. Dashed amber lines are estimates.
  4. Grey dashed circles are not yet sourced. Nothing goes on the map without a document.
Appearing on this map does not mean wrongdoing. It shows how Medicare money moves, as reported by government bodies, researchers and companies. Insurers’ responses are added next to critical findings as they are sourced.
Not on the map yet: insurers’ lobbying (Senate lobbying disclosures), political giving (Federal Election Commission), and Justice Department cases about Medicare Advantage billing. These will be added only with the filings themselves.

Key dates

What you can do

Dweller explains your options and drafts what you need. You decide and you send. Dweller never enrolls you or contacts a plan for you.

Fact · checked Nov 9, 2026
Oct 15 – Dec 7, 2026
Open Enrollment. Join, switch or drop a Medicare Advantage or drug plan, or go back to Original Medicare. New coverage starts Jan 1.
Jan 1 – Mar 31, 2027
MA Open Enrollment. Already in an MA plan? You can make one change: another MA plan, or Original Medicare plus a drug plan.
Any time, if something changes
Special Enrollment. For example, you move, lose other coverage, or your plan leaves your area.

Source: Medicare.gov, Understanding Medicare Advantage Plans.

Free help in North Carolina

SHIIP, NC Department of Insurance. Free and unbiased; they don’t sell insurance.

1-855-408-1212

Monday to Friday, 8am–5pm · ncdoi.gov/shiip

Leaving Medicare Advantage?

Check Medigap first. You are guaranteed a Medigap policy only in certain situations, such as your plan leaving your area or your first 12 months in Medicare Advantage. Medicare.gov: When can I buy Medigap?
