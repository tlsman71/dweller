# Privacy

These are Dweller's privacy commitments. They apply to the app and to field testing.

- **Every field is optional.** You can skip any question.
- **Export and delete.** You can download everything Dweller holds about you and delete it all.
- **Never sold.** Your data is never sold or rented.
- **Partners see totals only.** Nonprofit partners get anonymized, aggregate counts, never individual data.
- **Voice is transcribed, then deleted.** A text option is always available.
- **Avatars:** built from your answers, never from a face photo. If a photo option is ever added, the photo is cartoonized and the original deleted.
- **Reports and police tips:** Dweller drafts; you send from your own phone, email or the official form. Police-tip drafts are not kept after you send them. For emergencies, call 911.
- **This repository is public.** Anything posted in issues can be seen by anyone, so don't post personal details. Use DwellerApp@proton.me for anything private.
